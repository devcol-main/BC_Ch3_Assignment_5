 * Stages: A total of 3 levels.
 * Random Item Spawns: Health Potions, Big & Small Coins, and Mines randomly appear on the map.
 * Level Progression: You advance to the next stage once you collect all the coins spawned in the current level.
 * Health Recovery: Your health is fully restored upon advancing to a higher level.
 * Game Over: Triggered if you run out of time or your health drops to 0
 * Game Complete: Achieved by clearing all 3 stages.
